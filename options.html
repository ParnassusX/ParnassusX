Keyboard Shortcuts

You can assign keyboard shortcuts to quickly apply your presets via Chrome's extension shortcut settings page.

  • Apply First Preset: Activates the first preset in your alphabetically sorted list. (Default: Ctrl+Shift+1 or Command+Shift+1)
  • Apply Second Preset: Activates the second preset in your alphabetically sorted list. (Default: Ctrl+Shift+2 or Command+Shift+2)
  • Apply Third Preset: Activates the third preset in your alphabetically sorted list. (Default: Ctrl+Shift+3 or Command+Shift+3)
